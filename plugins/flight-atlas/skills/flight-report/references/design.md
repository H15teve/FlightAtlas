# Final template design contract

These rules reflect the completed template, not every superseded request.

- Flight-number TOP3: all three rows use 22 px, retaining gold #b78b35 / silver #7f8b97 / bronze #a86c46. Flight-number and count share rank color/size; ordinal is 4 px smaller. Keep row centers and count column aligned. Very long strings may shrink to fit, never clip counts.
- South China Sea inset: render native geographic geometry as SVG, not a screenshot. Register the unchanged China geometry under an alias to disable ECharts' automatic schematic inset and its extra frame; keep all native features, including Taiwan and maritime geometry. Retain only the deliberately placed outer frame of the geographic inset. Audit native/rendered region counts.

- Flight-number TOP3: below the frequent domestic route list, count all records including international flights. Shared rows use the user-verified main operating number; others use the exported number. Never accept a guessed shared-flight number. Preserve the exported number in the audit. Normalize case/whitespace, exclude missing numbers, keep leading zeroes and break ties alphabetically. The route threshold does not filter TOP3. Each flight row still counts once.

- A350 mask weight: use the A339 reference's approximate black-border thickness relative to windshield width, not its pane geometry. Preserve the A350 rounded rear pane and slanted front pane; do not enlarge the windshield to thicken its surround. Both use the same gray glass and continuous black outer mask.

- Aircraft projection (2026-10-04 revision): every built-in family uses a left-facing standard orthographic side elevation with a horizontal fuselage, no intentional yaw/pitch/roll or three-quarter perspective. Far-side engines/gear may be occluded; a four-engine aircraft may show only the two near-side nacelles. Preserve white contours around the fuselage, filled wings, tail and nacelles. Minor antennas are intentionally omitted; never invent dorsal or ventral spikes or far winglets on the fuselage roof. Physical geometry is still illustrative rather than engineering-certified. Never add visible engines merely to expose the total installed count.

- Passport: cream security-paper background, deep navy title, pastel aligned aircraft/IATA ribbon, borderless land-hatched world map expanded to [15,205,1570,820] on a 1600×2200 canvas; routes and markers; purpose-designed whole circular flags below. No passport metric footnotes. Identity codes left aligned; optional small script signature right of title. km/d/h use original light color/typeface at the number's size. MRZ-like filler distributed between identity groups, not just trailing chevrons.
- Atlas: FLIGHT ATLAS only in header, same navy title family; ATLAS +5.5% width, slightly heavier, wider tracking. Section titles equal size. Regional 5 cards/row, narrow trunk 4, wide trunk 3. Physical approximate aircraft length scale is shared (9 SVG px/metre), constrained by card height. Filled aircraft wings use white outlines; gray cockpit windows, body-colored engine intake. A350 retains a small outlined realistic mask. Mainstream family profiles: 738, A320ceo/neo, 789, A359, 77W, 744. Other families use a documented representative, with observed subtypes/counts in one line; illustrations aren't engineering drawings.
- Landing gear: keep tires and solid hub faces in fuselage color, with white structural relief. Use the representative subtype's single/two/three-axle arrangement and gear location, not one universal star-shaped hub texture. Nose and braked main hubs are separate structures. Far-side wheels may overlap in side projection; never expose every installed wheel by adding perspective. A340-300 has a centerline single-axle twin-wheel unit; A380 wing and body units have two and three axles respectively. Unverified vendor-specific bolt/hole counts are omitted, not invented; family illustrations are not registration-specific equipment records.
- Strict aircraft palette: regional body #7845a4, narrow-trunk #165c91, wide-trunk #c28316. One fixed flat body fill per group includes tires, connecting gear legs/struts/braces, engine intakes/exhausts, fuselage and wings. White #FFFFFF outlines/edges and solid cabin windows; gray #858585 cockpit glass only, with black mask on the selected A350/A330neo profiles, not default C919. No neutral metallic/gray fill elsewhere. User-approved deterministic PNG color normalization preserves alpha and role-color edge antialiasing; palette-audit.json and tests prevent color drift. This developer preparation step is not a runtime image-generation requirement.
- Editorial categories, not aerodrome A–F classes: Dash8/E190/E195/C909 and added regionals belong in regional. Family title E190 includes E195; Dash8 subtitle Dash8-Q400. Boeing subtype names include B prefix. Mainstream profiles retain their specified size; other families use most-flown subtype length when available (deterministic code-order ties). The fixed family artwork is scaled, not redrawn for every fuselage/window/wing variant. Selected representatives and approximation are recorded in the audit.
- Actual carriers: header OPERATING AIRLINES / 实际承运航司. Height and width both constrain local logos; Chinese-name logos preferred. Carriers below bar threshold go in a real-logo grid; singles aren't bars. Alliances below airline/map columns use built-in licensed logo+count, plain aircraft symbol for none/unknown. No alliance bars. Final reports require approved airline assets and featured/retired photos; placeholders are diagnostic-only, not an implicit fallback.
- LOGO placement principle: center and scale the actual artwork bounds, not the source image canvas. Exclude outer transparent margins from placement and size calculations. For raster assets use visible alpha bounds; measure rendered SVG alpha bounds with Sharp, then retain the original vector image in a fitted viewport. Do not assume the original viewBox is tight. Opaque background pixels are content; do not automatically erase white areas. Fit proportionally within both maximum width and maximum height, retain a small symmetric safety margin, and preserve the complete mark and its internal spacing. Apply the same rule to private and built-in assets; never compensate with airline-specific offsets, stretch the mark, or crop its content. Visually verify centering and displayed size in both bar rows and logo grids.
- Adaptive airline inventory: reserve a separate right-aligned count column with at least 20 px between the longest bar and the widest number. Up to eight bar rows use 64 px pitch; longer lists use 52 px. The below-threshold logo grid follows the bar content, four logos per row, rather than being pinned to the bottom of a sparse column. Unknown-carrier messages have a separate row. Airline and domestic-map containers retain a common height, expanding when either content requires it; never stretch logo geometry to fill white space.
- Demo-only logo exception: the public README demo uses the English AIR CHINA wordmark with the phoenix symbol by explicit user choice. This does not change the user-report rule: prefer complete logos containing Chinese names for mainland Chinese airlines. Do not copy the demo's exception into a user's asset-selection workflow.
- Domestic map: paper background, emphasized China geometry, Taiwan and a South China Sea inset; no South China Sea caption, no airport leader lines. Labels must be closest to their own point, not a nearby point; spacing checks for Pearl River Delta, CTU/TFU/CKG, PEK/PKX/SJW. All directed routes ≥threshold listed in clear space, no top-ten cut and no reciprocal merge in ranking.
- Airport visits: departure+arrival stacked bars; full Chinese display names with sensible international-airport shortening. Natural IATA spacing, code right aligned, CN names left; total and split numbers vertically centered with codes. Bars default total≥3. Adjacent word cloud shows every airport, exact v3 logarithmic sizing/seed/colors, no artificial IATA tracking. Both containers have the same vertical extent, chosen from bar-row count and full word-cloud packing needs instead of a fixed minimum of 1450 px. Sparse bars use larger names/codes/counts and thicker bars; row pitch is capped at 62 px (56 for dense lists), vertically centered rather than stretched to fill the cloud. Grow the shared area if necessary to preserve every cloud word.
- Repeated aircraft: registration, prefixed subtype, carrier/age on one row, featured most-flown photo card; other cards' group vertically centered beside it. Retired section: date/status between age/carrier and photo, equal-height cards. Photos maintain original aspect ratio, align horizontally to text width and retain watermark; attribution lives in sidecar rather than card footer.
- Missing data never looks like a factual zero. Preserve counts and record unverified carriers/types/durations/statuses in the audit. Unknown models need no freshly generated art.

- Script signatures preserve the user-provided case (`signature_name`, falling back to `name`). Fit and center measured glyph ink bounds proportionally inside the title-right box; the ink must not touch the passport title.
- Domestic map and South China Sea inset preserve geographic aspect ratio. Growing airline columns may increase the map container height, but must not stretch the geography. Embedded map SVGs use `xMidYMid meet`.
- Aircraft age labels use exact delivery days when verified; supported delivery intervals display approximate values (≈) or an age range. Fit long age ranges inside the reserved number box.
